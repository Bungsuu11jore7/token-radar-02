How the radar scores — model radar-2

implied value = TVL0.9 × 1.6 × growth × overdue × breadth × category

Growth7-day TVL change, clamped to −60…+200%, then a concave velocity. A +4,448% spike counts as fast, not as 44×.
OverdueDays running with no token recorded. A launch is likeliest inside a window around 700 days — not at the start, not never.
BreadthEach extra chain up to 8 adds 4%. A protocol live on many chains has more doors to farm through.
CategoryA small prior by family (0.92–1.06). Points programs cluster in Dexs, lending and derivatives; bridges and staked products rarely launch a token of their own.

What the snapshot cannot say. It carries no testnet activity, no points program and no fundraising record — so momentum, overdue age and category are proxies for airdrop probability, built from the figures the snapshot does carry. The board says a protocol records no token, never that it has none: a protocol on the list may still hold a token its DefiLlama record does not carry.
